Every sound your Mac plays, through your Audio Units.

Plug sits between your apps and your speakers. Pick everything or just the apps you want, run them through any Audio Unit effect on your Mac, and send the result to the output you choose.

What it does

System audio or chosen apps

Process everything, or tick only the apps you want. The rest play normally.

Any Audio Unit effect

Apple's built-in effects and your third-party plugins, each with its own controls window.

A chain you can rearrange

Drag effects into order and bypass any of them without losing its settings.

A master with headroom

From silence to +10 dB, with 0 dB marked by a bent tack. Link it to a MIDI knob in two clicks.

Presets

Save the whole setup (input, apps, output, effects and level) and switch in one click.

Always within reach

Start, stop and set the level from the Dock icon or the menu bar, where you can also add your own buttons that send MIDI, run scripts and Shortcuts, or ask Claude.

Install in a minute

  1. Open the Plug disk image and drag Plug onto Applications.
  2. The first time, right-click Plug in Applications and choose Open, then Open again.
  3. Press START and allow System Audio Recording when macOS asks.
